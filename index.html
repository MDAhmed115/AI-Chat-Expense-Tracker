      Step 1 of 3

      Connect your Google account

      Step 2 of 3

      Create a free key at Google AI Studio , then paste it below. The key is validated before it is saved in this browser.

      Step 3 of 3

      Connect your Google Sheet

      Choose an existing Expense Chat template spreadsheet in your connected Google account, or download and upload the template first.

      Download Template
      Upload and convert the template in Google Drive
      1. Download the Excel template to your device.
      2. Open Google Drive using the Google account connected in Step 1.
      3. Select New → File upload and upload Expense-Chat-Template.xlsx.
      4. Right-click the uploaded file and choose Open with → Google Sheets. Complete the conversion if prompted.
      5. Return here and click Select my Google Sheet. Choose the converted spreadsheet.

      Expense Chat validates the required tabs before linking the spreadsheet.
