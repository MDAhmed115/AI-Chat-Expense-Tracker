      Step 2 of 2

      Set up your Sheet

      Your transactions are saved in a Google Sheet in your Drive.

      Download Template
      Almost there — add the template to your Google Drive
      1. Download the Excel template to your device.
      2. Open Google Drive using the same Google account you connected above.
      3. Select New → File upload and upload the downloaded Expense-Chat-Template.xlsx.
      4. After uploading, right-click the file and choose Open with → Google Sheets. If prompted, save or convert it as a Google Sheets file.
      5. Return here and click Select my Google Sheet. Choose the converted spreadsheet.

      Once linked, Expense Chat can use that spreadsheet to store your transactions, budgets, and chat history.
